## Returns

You can return a product within 7 days of delivery when it arrived damaged, defective or different from what you ordered. Contact us with your order number and photos of the product.

## Refunds

Once we receive and check the returned product, we refund the product price to your bank account within 7 working days. Shipping costs are refunded when the mistake was ours.

## Not returnable

Digital products, personalized items and products marked as non-returnable can't be returned unless they are defective.
