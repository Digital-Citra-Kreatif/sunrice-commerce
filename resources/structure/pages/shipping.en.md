## Couriers and costs

We ship with the couriers shown at checkout. Shipping costs are calculated from the destination and the weight of your order and are shown before you pay.

## Processing time

Orders paid before 14:00 on working days are usually shipped the same day; others the next working day.

## Tracking

You receive the tracking number by email as soon as your order is shipped. You can also follow it on the order page.
