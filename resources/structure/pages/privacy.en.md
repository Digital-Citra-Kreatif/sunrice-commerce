## What we collect

When you order, register or contact us we collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and the details of your orders. If you pay by bank transfer, we keep the transfer confirmation you send us.

## Why

We use this data to process and deliver your orders, to send you order updates, to answer your questions and to meet our legal and tax obligations. We only send marketing messages when you have agreed to receive them.

## Who we share it with

We share your data only with the couriers and payment providers needed to complete your order. We never sell your data.

## How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your account at any time; your past orders are then anonymized.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting us.
